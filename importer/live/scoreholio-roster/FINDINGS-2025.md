Scoreholio's organizer view carries every player's **real name next to the
nickname**, because it has to know who people are in order to submit results to
DUPR. Every export we had ever pulled was the Match Log, which strips that out.

Swept all **280 tournaments from 2025** and read the roster from each.

## Why one night is short, and why that is the important finding

`Michael S.` is not one person. Scoreholio's own rosters say:

    2025-11-21  Michael S. = Michael Spezio
    2025-11-27  Michael S. = Michael Scancarello
    2025-12-04  Michael S. = BOTH men, same roster
    2025-12-05  Michael S. = Michael Spezio

`Danny M.` is the same story - Danny Miklin on two nights, Danny Marino on a
third.

So the handle means different people on different nights. **Any single global
answer to "who is Michael S." would have been wrong**, and would have quietly
handed one man a chunk of another man's record. Resolving per tournament is
correct; resolving per handle is not.

That leaves exactly one genuine hole: on **2025-12-04 both Michaels played and
both used "Michael S."** - 19 games that cannot be attributed from the data
alone. Flagged, not guessed.

## It confirmed work we had done the hard way

Thirteen aliases we had derived from week-by-week win/loss fingerprints match
Scoreholio's records exactly - `Chase Gold` -> Sal Farruggia, `BReady` ->
Jarred Barretti, `Andy` -> Andrew Heyman, `Juju` -> Julio Olivo, `French Mike`
-> Mikel Strauch, `Ron D.` -> Eran Dekel and others. Two independent methods,
same answers.

## It also answers open duplicate questions

Four cases where Scoreholio's real name differs from the roster row we had
guessed at - each one resolves a merge that has been sitting open:

    Tim L    -> "Timothy Lynch"   (we hold Tim Lynch AND Timothy Lynch apart)
    Chris P. -> "Chris Paolina"   (we hold Chris AND Christopher Paolina apart)
    Tori     -> "Wiktoria W"      (Tori is short for Wiktoria)
    Chase Gold -> "Sal Farruggia" (consistent with the merge already confirmed)

These are evidence, not applied. Merging two identities is the one thing this
project never does on inference alone.

## Not collected

Player email addresses are present in the same records and were deliberately
left out. The stats site has no use for 500 people's emails.
